# 📍 Where to buy?

**Cosmic Meta NFTs can be minted on our** [**website**](https://mint.cosmicmeta.io/)**.** Connect your Web 3.0 wallet to the DApp while on the Ethereum blockchain by simply clicking on "**Connect wallet**" button.&#x20;

After your wallet is connected you can choose how many NFTs you would like to mint. Then confirm your transaction and gas fees and in a few seconds your transaction will be completed. You will find your NFT on your [Opensea ](https://opensea.io/)profile page.

**Also, you can purchase already minted NFTs on the secondary market on** [**OpenSea.**](https://opensea.io/collection/octopeeps-kingdoms)

Please also check out [Broken link](broken-reference "mention")for other NFT marketplaces that you can buy Cosmic Meta NFTs.
